The [Vajrachedikā reading companion](https://vajrachhedika.arybszleger.com/)
presents the *Vajra Cutter Sutra* — the Diamond Sutra, as it is often known in
English — as a reader designed for actual recitation and study rather than
casual browsing.

## Three languages, one text

The sutra is shown in **Tibetan, English and Sanskrit**, so that a reader can
recite in one language while keeping an eye on another. For practitioners who
recite the sutra regularly, the parallel presentation makes it natural to
gradually absorb the Tibetan while understanding what is being said.

## Built for recitation

A few features are aimed squarely at how this text is actually used:

- **Adjustable-speed auto-scroll** — set a pace and recite hands-free.
- **Tilt-to-scroll** — nudge the page along without touching the screen.
- **Light and dark mode** — for shrine rooms and late evenings alike.
- **An index** — jump directly to any section of the sutra.
- **A glossary of terms** — the technical vocabulary of the sutra, explained.

## Why this sutra

The *Vajra Cutter* holds a special place across the Tibetan Buddhist world:
recited for the accumulation of merit, studied for its uncompromising
presentation of emptiness, and kept in homes as an object of veneration in
its own right. Making it easier to read — in three languages, at your own
pace — felt like a worthwhile offering.

The app is free, like everything on this site. [Open the reading
companion](https://vajrachhedika.arybszleger.com/) and try a few pages.
